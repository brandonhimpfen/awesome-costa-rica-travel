> A curated list of resources for traveling in Costa Rica, covering official portals, transport, region guides, national parks, and digital nomad resources.

Costa Rica is a biodiverse Central American destination celebrated for its lush rainforest reserves, active volcanoes, Pacific and Caribbean coastlines, and pioneering ecotourism initiatives. This list is intended for eco-travelers, wildlife conservationists, surf enthusiasts, and nature explorers.

## Official & Planning

Essential government portals, park reservation platforms, migration requirements, and weather tracking.

- [Visit Costa Rica (ICT)](https://www.visitcostarica.com/) - Official tourism board portal offering destination guides, entry requirements, and itinerary advice.
- [SINAC Online Passports & Tickets](https://serviciosenlinea.sinac.go.cr/) - Official national park reservation platform for purchasing advance entry passes to Manuel Antonio, Poás, Tenorio, and other protected areas.
- [Dirección General de Migración y Extranjería](https://www.migracion.go.cr/) - Official portal for visa regulations, entry requirements, and immigration procedures.
- [IMN Costa Rica (Instituto Meteorológico Nacional)](https://www.imn.ac.cr/) - Official meteorological institute providing microclimate forecasts, volcanic weather alerts, and rain radar maps.

## Transportation

Navigating domestic flights, shuttle buses, public transit, and rental vehicles across Costa Rica.

- [Sansa Airlines](https://flysansa.com/) - Primary domestic airline operating regional flights between San José and coastal airstrips.
- [Costa Rica Green Airways](https://www.costaricagreenairways.com/) - Domestic eco-focused airline serving Tambor, Quepos, Drake Bay, and Nosara.
- [Interbus Costa Rica](https://www.interbusonline.com/) - Shared and private tourist shuttle service covering major hub-to-hub destinations across the country.
- [Mepe Bus Company](https://mepe.co.cr/) - Main public bus operator serving routes between San José, Puerto Viejo, and the Southern Caribbean coast.
- [Costa Rica Bus Schedules](https://thebusschedule.com/cr/) - Comprehensive route guide and schedule planner for public intercity bus lines.

## Destinations & Regions

### San José & Central Valley

- [San José City Tourism Guide](https://www.sanjose.gob.cr/) - Official capital city guide detailing historic theaters, museums, and local markets.
- [Poás Volcano National Park Info](https://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/accvc/pnvp/) - Official safety procedures, visitor updates, and reservation guidelines for Poás crater views.

### Guanacaste & Nicoya Peninsula

- [Guanacaste Tourism Hub](https://www.visitcostarica.com/en/costa-rica/where-to-go/guanacaste) - Regional guide for Gold Coast beaches, surfing spots, and Liberia airport connections.
- [Santa Teresa & Montezuma Info](https://www.visitcostarica.com/en/costa-rica/where-to-go/nicoya-peninsula) - Guide for exploring the southern Nicoya Peninsula's surf breaks, tide pools, and nature reserves.

### La Fortuna & Northern Highlands

- [Arenal Volcano & La Fortuna Portal](https://lafortuna.cr/) - Visitor guide for volcano treks, geothermal hot springs, and zip-lining adventures.
- [Monteverde Reserve Official Site](https://www.reservamonteverde.com/) - Visitor portal for exploring the cloud forest, canopy walkways, and guided night tours.

### Central & South Pacific

- [Manuel Antonio National Park Guide](https://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/acopac/pnma/) - Trail information, wildlife rules, and reservation notices for the popular park.
- [Osa Peninsula & Corcovado Guide](https://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/acosa/pnc/) - Expedition requirements, certified guide directories, and ranger station itineraries for Corcovado National Park.

### Caribbean Coast

- [Tortuguero National Park Info](https://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/act/pnt/) - Canal navigation routes, sea turtle nesting season calendars, and village visitor info.
- [Puerto Viejo & Southern Caribbean](https://www.visitcostarica.com/en/costa-rica/where-to-go/caribbean) - Travel guide for Cahuita, Manzanillo, Afro-Caribbean culture, and coastal reserves.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and foreign remote workers taking advantage of Costa Rica's Digital Nomad law.

- [Costa Rica Digital Nomad Visa (Migración)](https://www.migracion.go.cr/Paginas/Estancia-para-Trabajadores-y-Prestadores-de-Servicios-Remotos.aspx) - Official application portal and eligibility requirements for the 1-year remote worker visa.
- [Outsite Costa Rica](https://www.outsite.co/locations/costa-rica) - Co-living and co-working hubs located in popular remote-work spots like Santa Teresa and Puerto Viejo.

## Safety & Nature Regulations

Practical advice on emergency contacts, wildlife protection rules, and water safety.

- [Costa Rica Emergency Services (911)](https://www.911.go.cr/) - National 911 dispatch hotline for police, medical, and fire emergencies.
- [ICT Tourist Police Direct Contact](https://www.ict.go.cr/en/services-to-tourists/tourist-police.html) - Specialist tourist assistance division for safety guidance and precinct support.
- [Stop Animal Swelfies Campaign](https://www.mivae.go.cr/) - Ministry of Environment guidelines detailing legal restrictions against feeding or disturbing wild animals for photos.
